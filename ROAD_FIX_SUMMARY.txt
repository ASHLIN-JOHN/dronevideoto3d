HOW IT WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. MAIN ROAD SPINE
   - Creates straight vertical road through center
   - 7 connected segments
   - Forms backbone of entire network
   - Z-axis aligned for depth

2. BRANCHING ROADS
   - Detects left/right turns from video analysis
   - Creates left branch at 45° angle
   - Creates right branch at -45° angle
   - 3 segments per branch
   - Properly spaced and aligned

3. INTERSECTIONS
   - Detects horizontal roads in analysis
   - Creates 4-way crossing
   - Extends roads to both sides
   - Perpendicular alignment

4. CURVES & WINDING
   - Detects winding/curved roads
   - Creates S-curve pattern
   - Smooth transitions
   - Maintains alignment

5. CONNECTION SNAPPING
   - Aligns roads to nearby buildings
   - Snaps to grid for clean alignment
   - Professional appearance
