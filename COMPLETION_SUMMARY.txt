TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Issue: Projects not showing
→ Solution: Click Projects tab, check browser storage enabled

Issue: Models not displaying
→ Solution: Ensure Models directory has .glb/.gltf files

Issue: Video analysis fails
→ Solution: Use MP4 format, keep video 1-2 minutes

Issue: App won't start
→ Solution: Check Python 3.8+ installed, PyQt5 available

See QUICK_START.md for more troubleshooting.
